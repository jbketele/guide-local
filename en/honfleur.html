Honfleur

Between port, art and Norman charm

Presentation

Honfleur,
an timeless charm

Honfleur is one of the gems of the Flowered Coast. Around the Old Harbor, its colorful houses, cobbled streets and maritime heritage compose a unique setting. A city of art and history, Honfleur has also inspired many painters and invites visitors today to stroll through its quays, galleries and terraces.
