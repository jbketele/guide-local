Cabourg

Presentation

Cabourg, the elegance
by the sea

Iconic seaside resort of the Fleurie Coast, Cabourg seduces with its long sandy beach, its Belle Époque villas and its peaceful atmosphere. Between beach walks, city center and heritage, the city is easy to discover on foot.
