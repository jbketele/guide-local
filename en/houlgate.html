Houlgate

The Belle Époque resort facing the sea

Presentation

Houlgate,
elegant and preserved

Between Belle Époque villas, sandy beaches and the cliffs of the Black Cows, Houlgate has a unique charm on the Flowered Coast. The resort invites you to stroll through its streets, admire its architectural heritage and enjoy its coastline, for a walk or a day by the sea.

Discover the many villas that testify to the golden age of the resort. Houlgate has a particularly rich architectural heritage, with nearly 500 remarkable villas.
