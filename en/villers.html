Villers sur Mer

Between sea, nature and jurassic heritage

Presentation

Villers sur Mer,
between land and sea

Villers-sur-Mer seduces with its seafront, its seaside villas and its exceptional natural heritage. Between the beach, the cliffs of the Black Cows and the coastal marsh, the resort offers a nice balance between walking, nature and discovery. It is also an essential destination for paleontology enthusiasts thanks to Paléospace, dedicated to the history of the Jurassic in Normandy.
